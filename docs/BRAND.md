> **Status: draft for owner approval.** Written by Claude from the product plan (CLAUDE.md,
> docs/PRD.md) and the look already used on the landing, sign-in and app pages. The product name

---

## 1. Who we are

**One line:** ConvoPilot is an AI chief of staff that turns your professional conversations into
clear replies, commitments and follow-ups.

**Who it is for (first):** founders and operators at small companies who handle many important
conversations — investors, customers, hires, partners — and drop follow-ups because there is too
much to track.

**The promise:** *Nothing important slips, and every reply still sounds like you.*

**What makes us different**

1. **Your voice, not a template.** Drafts sound like the user, never like corporate filler.
2. **Evidence for everything.** Every answer points to the message it came from, or says
   plainly that there is no record.
3. **You stay in charge.** The AI drafts and proposes; the user approves. Nothing is sent for them.
4. **Private by design.** Each account is its own private workspace.

## 2. Personality

ConvoPilot behaves like an excellent, calm chief of staff:

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Calm and organised | Hyped or frantic |
| Direct and plain-spoken | Vague, jargon-heavy |
| Quietly confident | Boastful ("revolutionary", "10x") |
| Discreet and trustworthy | Creepy or "always watching" |
| Helpful, then out of the way | Chatty or needy |

## 3. Voice and tone

**Rules**

- Write like a sharp colleague talking to a busy founder: short sentences, everyday words.
- Lead with the benefit to the reader, then the detail.
- Be specific: "Reply drafted in your style" beats "AI-powered communication workflows".
- Say what the product does *not* do when it builds trust ("It never sends anything for you").
- Never overpromise. No invented numbers, testimonials or customer logos.
- Use "you" for the user and "we" for the team. Contractions are fine (we're, you'll).
- British/Indian or US spelling: pick one and stay consistent — **default: US spelling**.

**Tone by situation**

| Situation | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing | Warm, confident, concrete | "Your AI chief of staff for every important conversation." |
| Product UI | Short, neutral, action-first | "Draft reply", "Save contact", "Nothing here yet" |
| Errors | Calm, blame-free, tells you what to do next | "This sign-in link has expired. Request a new link to continue." |
| Security / privacy | Plain and exact, no fear | "Your account is a private workspace. Other users can't see your data." |
| Emails | Personal, brief, one clear action | "Here's your invitation to ConvoPilot. This link works once." |

**Words we use:** follow-up, commitment, reply, draft, conversation, approve, evidence, private,
in your own voice, chief of staff.

**Words we avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, 10x, synergy, leverage, seamless, magic,
"AI-powered" as the headline, unlock, supercharge, disrupt, guaranteed.

## 4. Messaging

**Primary tagline:** Your AI chief of staff for every important conversation.

**Alternatives to test:**

- Never drop a follow-up again.
- Turn conversations into commitments — and keep them.
- Reply in your voice. Remember every promise.

**Elevator pitch (30 seconds):** Founders lose deals and goodwill through forgotten follow-ups.
ConvoPilot reads the email threads you choose, tells you what the other person wants, drafts a
reply in your own voice and keeps track of what you promised — with evidence for everything and
nothing sent without your approval.

**Call to action:** "Request early access" (before launch) · "Accept your invitation" (invite
email) · "Sign in".

## 5. Logo (temporary)

- Current mark: the letter **C** in white on an indigo rounded square, followed by the word
  **ConvoPilot** in semibold.
- Clear space: at least the height of the "C" on every side. Minimum size: 24 px mark.
- Do not stretch, recolour outside the palette, add effects or place on busy images.
- **Later:** commission a proper logo (wordmark + icon) once the name is final (see §10).

## 7. Typography

- **Typeface:** Geist (sans) for everything; Geist Mono only for codes or technical values.
- **Scale:** page title 30–48 px bold · section heading 24–30 px bold · card heading 16 px
  semibold · body 14–16 px · small print 12 px.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Request early access", not "Request Early Access").

## 8. Interface style

- Clean, spacious, white cards with thin borders and soft rounded corners (12–16 px).
- Every screen shows loading, empty, error and success states — written in the brand voice.
- Imported conversations always appear as plain text; no remote images.
- Icons (when added): simple outline style, one family only.
- Motion: subtle and functional only.

## 9. Email style

- Sender name: **ConvoPilot**; from address on our own domain (e.g. `hello@` / `no-reply@`).
- Subject: plain and specific ("Your ConvoPilot sign-in link", "You're invited to ConvoPilot").
- Body: 2–4 short lines, one button, a note on expiry, no tracking pixels, no images required.

## 10. Open brand decisions (owner)

1. **Name.** `convopilot.com` (registered 2024) and `convopilot.ai` are already owned by someone
   else. Before investing in the name, check whether another company uses "ConvoPilot"
   (trademark risk). Options: keep the name with an available domain, or choose a new name.
2. **Domain** (see the owner guidance given in session, recorded in docs/WORKLOG.md).
3. **Logo** by a designer once the name is final.
4. **Spelling convention** (default US).
