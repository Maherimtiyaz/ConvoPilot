## 1. Audit of v0.1 (what we kept, what we changed)

| v0.1 decision | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning: AI chief of staff, evidence, your voice, you approve | **Kept** | Genuinely different and true to the product. It is the strategic core. |
| Indigo `#4F46E5` as primary | **Replaced** | It is Tailwind's default accent: the most common colour in AI/SaaS. Recognisable as "template", not as us. |
| "C" in a rounded square | **Replaced** | A placeholder lettermark; hundreds of identical marks; carries no meaning. |
| Geist typeface | **Replaced** | Strongly associated with another company's platform; neutral but not ownable. |
| Voice rules (calm, direct, no hype) | **Kept and deepened** | Right for a skeptical, time-poor audience. |
| No signature graphic device | **Gap filled** | Nothing made v0.1 recognisable without its name. |
| No dark theme, tokens, prompts, misuse rules | **Gap filled** | Needed for a production-ready system. |

**What makes ConvoPilot genuinely different** (and therefore what the identity must express):
1. **Evidence.** Every answer is quoted and cited from the real conversation.
2. **Judgement.** It picks out what matters (commitments, asks, deadlines) like a chief of staff.
3. **Control.** It drafts and proposes; the person approves; nothing is sent.

## 2. Creative direction — "The Highlighted Quote"

A chief of staff reads everything and hands you the one line that matters, highlighted and
quoted. That single gesture — **highlighting the quote** — is the brand. It unites evidence
(quotation), judgement (highlighting), and calm editorial clarity (ink on paper).

Directions considered and rejected:
- **"Copilot / navigation"** (compass, arrow, wings): overused across AI products, and the
  "pilot" metaphor implies the AI is in control — the opposite of our promise.
- **"Conversation"** (speech bubbles, chat dots): the most common symbol in communication software.
- **"AI magic"** (sparkles, gradients, orbs): signals hype and unpredictability; erodes trust.

**Core values**
1. **Evidence over guesswork.** If we can't cite it, we don't claim it.
2. **Your voice, your call.** We draft; you decide.
3. **Discretion by default.** Private workspaces; we read only what you give us.
4. **Calm under load.** Clear, quiet, never noisy.
5. **Close the loop.** Every commitment ends in done, declined or delegated.

**Positioning statement.** For founders and small-team operators who juggle high-stakes
conversations, ConvoPilot is the AI chief of staff that turns threads into replies, commitments
and follow-ups. Unlike general AI writing tools and inbox assistants, it shows the evidence behind
every suggestion, writes in your own voice, and never acts without your approval.

**Unique value proposition.** Every reply in your voice. Every promise tracked. Every answer cited.

**Audience psychology.** Time-starved; reputation is their currency; skeptical of AI hype; afraid
of sounding robotic and of software acting on their behalf; want leverage without losing
authenticity. They trust products that are precise, calm and honest about limits.

**Competitive differentiation (by category).**
- *General AI writers:* write well but forget commitments and sound generic → we remember, cite, and match your voice.
- *Inbox speed tools:* optimise for clearing email → we optimise for not dropping what matters.
- *CRMs / relationship tools:* require manual data entry → we extract context from the conversations themselves, with your approval.

**Perception goals.** People should describe ConvoPilot as *sharp, trustworthy, calm, built for
serious operators*. Never as *a toy, hype, creepy, or another chatbot*.

## 4. Verbal identity

**Tagline:** **Nothing important slips.**
**Descriptor:** The AI chief of staff for your conversations.
Alternatives kept for testing: "Every conversation, followed through." · "Read less. Miss nothing."

**Messaging hierarchy**
1. Promise — Nothing important slips.
2. Three pillars, each with proof:
   - **Understand** — what they want and expect, each point quoted from the thread.
   - **Reply** — drafts in your own voice; edit, save, copy.
   - **Follow through** — commitments and tasks proposed for your approval.
3. Trust layer — private workspace · nothing sent for you · you approve every fact.
4. Call to action — Request early access (pre-launch) · Accept your invitation · Sign in.

**Tone of voice:** a calm, excellent chief of staff. Brief, specific, warm, never servile.

**Communication principles**
1. Lead with the outcome for the reader.
2. Be specific — name the thing ("Reply drafted", not "Content generated").
3. Show the evidence — quote, cite, or say "no record".
4. Say what we don't do when it builds trust ("It never sends anything for you").
5. No hype, no fear, no exclamation marks in product copy.
6. Sentence case everywhere. US spelling. Contractions welcome.

**Words we use:** follow-up, commitment, promise, reply, draft, approve, evidence, source, cited,
private, in your voice, chief of staff, next step, close the loop.
**Words we avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, 10x, magic, supercharge, unlock, seamless,
synergy, leverage, disrupt, effortless, "AI-powered" as a headline, guaranteed, autopilot.

| Situation | Write | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | "Nothing important slips." | "Supercharge your inbox with AI magic!" |
| Feature | "Every answer links to the message it came from." | "Advanced AI insights." |
| Empty state | "No conversations yet. Paste a thread to start." | "Oops! Nothing to see here 😅" |
| Error | "This link has expired. Request a new one." | "Error 403: OTP invalid." |
| Privacy | "Your workspace is private. Other users can't see it." | "Bank-grade military encryption!" |
| Email | "Here's your invitation. This link works once." | "🚀 You're IN!!!" |

**The name in text:** "ConvoPilot" (camel case) in sentences; the logo wordmark is lowercase.

## 5. Logo identity

### 5.1 Territories evaluated

| Territory | Distinctive | Meaningful | Small-size | Survives a rename | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark only | Low | Low | Poor | No | Rejected as primary |
| Lettermark "C" | Low | Low | Good | No | Rejected (generic) |
| Abstract symbol | Medium | Low | Good | Yes | Rejected (no story) |
| Geometric symbol (compass/arrow) | Low | Medium | Good | Yes | Rejected (overused, wrong metaphor) |
| Custom typographic identity | Medium | Medium | Poor | No | Supporting role only |
| **Combination mark: the Highlighted Quote** | **High** | **High** | **Good** | **Yes** | **Selected** |

### 5.2 The concept
- **Two closing quotation marks** = two people in a conversation, and the *quoted source*
  (evidence).
- **A chisel-cut highlighter stroke** behind them = judgement: picking out what matters.
- **The 20° lean** shared by the chisel ends and the quote tails = forward motion, follow-through.
- **Ink on marker yellow** = a printed briefing with the key line highlighted: editorial,
  credible, human.

Recognisable without the name: a yellow slanted bar holding two dark quote marks. No other
element of the identity is needed to identify it.

### 5.3 Construction (exact; see `scripts/brand/build_logo.py`)
- Symbol box **72 × 44** units. Chisel: a parallelogram whose ends lean **20° (tan 0.36)**:
  points (15.84, 0), (72, 0), (56.16, 44), (0, 44).
- Quote form: circle **r = 6.8**; straight tail to a tip **2.3 r** below and **1.55 r** left of
  the centre; edges tangent to the circle. Pair gap **3.2**; the pair is centred by its bounding box.
- Wordmark: lowercase **Instrument Sans SemiBold**, tracking **−1.5%**, converted to outlines.
  (A chisel-shaped tittle on the "i" was tested and rejected: it read as an accent.)
- Horizontal lockup: symbol height = **1.12 × ascender**, sitting on the baseline; gap =
  **0.42 × symbol height**.
- Mono versions knock the quotes out of the chisel (even-odd), so they work in one colour.

### 5.4 Logo architecture and when to use each

| File (`public/brand/`) | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `logo-horizontal.svg` (primary) | Website header, documents, decks, email footers — the default. |
| `logo-horizontal-on-dark.svg` | The same on Ink backgrounds. |
| `logo-vertical.svg` (secondary) | Square or centred layouts: splash screens, event badges, cover slides. |
| `symbol.svg` (icon-only) | Where the brand is already named nearby, or space is tight (≥ 24 px). |
| `wordmark-ink.svg` / `wordmark-white.svg` | Partner logo walls and legal lines where the symbol would crowd. |
| `logo-horizontal-mono-ink.svg` | One-colour printing, embossing, faxes, black-and-white documents. |
| `logo-horizontal-mono-white.svg` / `symbol-mono-white.svg` | Over photography or any single dark colour. |
| `app-icon.svg` | iOS/Android/PWA icon (1024 master; platform masks apply). |
| `favicon.svg` | Browser tab (quotes only — the chisel is too fine below 24 px). |
| `avatar.svg` | LinkedIn, X, GitHub, Product Hunt profile pictures (circle-crop safe). |

**Clear space:** at least **half the symbol height** on every side. **Minimum size:** horizontal
logo 96 px wide (25 mm in print); symbol 24 px (use the favicon below that).

## 8. Graphic language

1. **The Highlight** (signature): a chisel-cut Marker bar behind 1–3 words of a headline. One per
   composition. Never on body text. (`.cp-highlight` in `tokens.css`.)
2. **Citations**: mono tags that point to sources — `[1]`, `↳ Priya · 12 Mar, 09:14`. Wherever we
   claim something, we show where it came from.
3. **The Thread line**: a 1.5 px Ink line connecting a quote card to the commitment it produced —
   the visual story of "conversation → follow-through". Right angles only.
4. **The 20° lean**: reserved for the chisel and quote tails. Do not slant type or photos.
5. **Quote field pattern**: quote forms on a 20° lean grid, Ink at 6% on Paper, for social
   backgrounds and slide covers only.
6. **Shapes and borders**: rectangles, 1 px Ink 200 borders, radii 2–16 px (§9). Sharp beats bubbly.
7. **Iconography**: Lucide (ISC licence), 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, Ink 700. No filled or
   multicolour icons, no emoji in UI.
8. **Illustration**: none. We show the real product and real text; diagrams use the Thread line.
9. **Photography**: real working moments — desks, notebooks, phones in hand, natural light,
   slightly cool grade, shallow depth, space left for a headline. No staged handshakes, no
   people smiling at laptops, no robots, no glowing brains.
10. **Layout**: 12-column grid, generous white space, left-aligned text, one idea per section,
    evidence placed next to the claim it supports.

## 9. Design system (implementation)

Tokens: `docs/brand/tokens.json` (W3C design-token format) and `docs/brand/tokens.css`.

- **Colour tokens:** semantic `bg, surface, surface-muted, border, border-strong, text,
  text-muted, link, action-bg, action-text, highlight, highlight-soft, focus` for light and dark.
- **Spacing:** 4 px base — 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128.
- **Grid:** 12 columns, 24 px gutter, 1200 px container; margins 80 / 32 / 16 (desktop / tablet / phone).
- **Radius:** xs 2 (tags) · sm 6 (buttons, inputs) · md 10 (cards) · lg 16 (dialogs) · pill (status chips only).
- **Shadow:** 1 (resting cards) · 2 (menus) · 3 (dialogs). Ink-tinted, never black.
- **Components:** primary button = Ink fill, white text (dark mode: Marker fill, Ink text);
  secondary = white with Ink 300 border; link = Route Blue, underline on hover; inputs 44 px tall,
  6 px radius, Route focus ring 2 px; cards white, 1 px Ink 200 border, 10 px radius; citation
  chip = Ink 100 fill, mono 12 px; highlight = Marker 100 fill behind quoted text.
- **Responsive:** breakpoints 640 / 1024 / 1280; navigation collapses to a top bar under 1024;
  touch targets ≥ 44 px.
- **Themes:** light (default) and dark (Ink background). Marker yellow remains the accent in both.

## 12. Usage guidelines

**Do**
- Use the supplied SVGs; never redraw or retype the logo.
- Keep clear space ≥ half the symbol height; respect minimum sizes.
- Put the full-colour logo on White, Paper or Ink only.
- Use the mono versions on photos or single-colour production.
- Keep Marker to ≤ 5% of any composition; use it only as a fill behind Ink.
- Pair every claim with evidence (quote or citation) in marketing and product.

**Don't**
- Recolour the chisel (no blue, purple, gradients) or the quotes (no yellow quotes on white).
- Put the full-colour symbol on a yellow background — use the avatar version instead.
- Rotate, straighten or flip the chisel; change the 20° lean; stretch or squash.
- Add outlines, shadows, glows, 3D or animation effects to the static logo.
- Set the wordmark in another font or in "ConvoPilot" capitals.
- Use yellow text, yellow lines or yellow icons on light backgrounds (contrast 1.4:1).
- Use more than one Highlight per composition, or highlight whole sentences.
- Use stock imagery of robots, brains, sparkles or people pointing at screens.

**Cross-platform consistency checklist:** correct logo file · Ink/Paper/Marker proportions · two
type families max · one Highlight · evidence visible · sentence case · voice rules from §4.

## 16. Motion principles
Calm and purposeful: things are underlined, quoted and connected. Ease `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`;
160 ms for controls, 280 ms for cards, 480 ms for highlights. No bounce, spin or parallax. Every
animation is decorative-only, has a complete resting state, and is disabled under
`prefers-reduced-motion`; anything that loops longer than 5 s can be paused.
