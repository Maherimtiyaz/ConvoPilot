01 · The product in one gesture

From a thread to a kept promise.

This is the moment the whole brand is built on. A message arrives, ConvoPilot highlights what is being asked, shows where it came from, and proposes a commitment. Nothing becomes a task, and nothing is sent, until you approve it.

For founders who juggle high-stakes conversations, ConvoPilot turns threads into replies, commitments and follow-ups, with the evidence behind every suggestion and nothing done without approval.

05 · Colour

Ink, paper, and one highlighter.

Ink is print and discretion. Marker is attention, the one thing worth noticing. Paper is calm. Route blue is the path to act. Select any swatch to copy its code.

Paper 40%
White 25%
Ink 25%
Neutrals, tints and status

Marker is always a fill behind Ink, never text or lines on light backgrounds (1.4:1). Keep it under 5% of any layout: scarcity is what makes it mean "this matters". Every text pairing here passes WCAG AA.

07 · Graphic language and motion

Quote it. Highlight it. Connect it.

Three devices carry the brand everywhere, and one motion principle: things are underlined, quoted and connected. Movement is calm and purposeful, never bouncy.

The Highlight

Every promise, kept.

Chisel-cut marker behind one to three words. One per layout. Sweeps in left to right in 480 ms.

Citations

They expect the revised deck by Friday. [1]

↳ Priya · 12 Mar, 09:14

If we claim it, we show where it came from.

The Thread line
“Deck by Friday?”Send deck · Fri

A 1.5 px line from quote to commitment. Right angles only.

Motion

Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). 160 ms for controls, 280 ms for cards, 480 ms for highlights. Respects reduced motion.

Quote field6–8% Ink on Paper. Social and covers only.
Imagery and icons

Real working moments in natural light: desks, notebooks, a phone in hand. No robots, glowing brains or people smiling at laptops. Lucide outline icons at 1.75 px. No emoji in the product.

11 · Rules and audit

Does every touchpoint pass?

Premium means restraint, precision and evidence, not black and gold. Modern means flat geometry that ages slowly, not gradients. The idea, a highlighted quote, is what makes it ours.

Always
  • Use the supplied SVG files
  • Clear space of half the symbol's height
  • Logo on White, Paper or Ink
  • Marker under 5%, as a fill behind Ink
  • One Highlight per layout
  • Two type families per view at most
  • Evidence beside every claim
Never
  • Recolour, rotate, stretch or shadow the logo
  • Full-colour symbol on yellow
  • Yellow text, lines or icons on light
  • Retype the wordmark
  • Highlight whole sentences
  • Robots, brains, sparkles or stock pointing
  • Hype words
